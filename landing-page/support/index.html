Getting Started

AudioRouterNow lives in your menu bar — it has no Dock icon and no main window. After launching, look for the AudioRouterNow icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen and click it to open the control panel.

  1. Click the AudioRouterNow icon in the menu bar.
  2. Select the output devices you want to hear at the same time (e.g. speakers + headphones + your audio interface).
  3. Press Start. On first start, macOS asks for permission to capture system audio — this is required and is explained below.
  4. Your Mac's sound now plays through every selected device simultaneously. Your volume keys keep working.

Requirements

  • macOS 14.4 (Sonoma) or later — required for Apple's Process Tap API.
  • Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

Troubleshooting

No sound from the selected devices?

  • Make sure routing is actually running — the status line at the top of the menu tells you the current state.
  • Confirm each device you expect is checked in the menu and physically connected / powered on.
  • Check the volume on each individual output device (some interfaces and speakers have their own hardware volume).

macOS didn't ask for permission, or capture isn't working

AudioRouterNow needs the System Audio Recording permission to route sound. If you declined it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone / Audio Recording and enable AudioRouterNow, then start routing again. Nothing is ever recorded or stored — the permission is only used to capture audio in real time.

Some apps produce no sound (Apple Music Lossless, Netflix, TV+)

Certain services use DRM that blocks any app from capturing their audio. This is a macOS limitation, not a bug in AudioRouterNow, and it cannot be worked around.

Bluetooth and wired outputs sound slightly out of sync

Bluetooth devices (e.g. AirPods) add ~150–200 ms of codec latency. For music this is generally unnoticeable; for video, lip sync may be slightly off when mixing Bluetooth with wired outputs.

The app doesn't launch at login (or launches when I don't want it to)

Launch at login is entirely optional and off by default. Toggle "Launch AudioRouterNow at Login" in the menu at any time to turn it on or off. You can also manage it in System Settings → General → Login Items.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AudioRouterNow really free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads and no subscription. An optional Tip Jar (Coffee ☕ $1.99 / Beer 🍺 $4.99) is available if you'd like to support development. It's a one-time, entirely optional purchase.

Does it record or send my audio anywhere?

No. All audio processing happens locally on your Mac. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why does it need audio-recording permission?

To route sound to multiple outputs, the app must capture the system audio stream in real time using Apple's Process Tap API. This capture is never saved and never leaves your device.

Can I change my output devices while routing?

Yes. Add or remove devices from the menu at any time — routing adjusts on the fly without needing a restart.
